On success, immediately calls `response.delete_cookie(_STATE_COOKIE, path="/", domain=settings.session_cookie_domain)`. If `pkce_required`, does the equivalent for `_PKCE_COOKIE` (raise 400 on missing/invalid, log `auth.<provider>.pkce_cookie_missing`/`invalid`, delete on success) and returns the `code_verifier`; otherwise returns `None`. **Both cookies are deleted on the `response` object as soon as they've been read and verified — regardless of what happens later in the handler** (this is what makes `state` single-use; see design.md's cookie-clearing risk note).
